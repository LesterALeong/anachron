# Candidate metadata contract

This is a future local metadata contract, not an arXiv upload form. A metadata
generator may run only from a complete verified v4 candidate receipt and the
frozen generated-only manuscript template. It must bind title, author,
abstract, AI-assistance disclosure, category selection, and the explicit v3
inclusion count of zero to the candidate receipt. Its abstract is generated
from the answer-free projection, including the one frozen positive, zero, or
negative result sentence form selected by the verified paired direction.

It must not expose raw evidence, terminal answers, v3 results, source-audit
rationales, authorization records, reviews, approvals, or outreach text.
Generating local metadata does not authorize researcher contact,
endorsement-code transmission, upload, or submission. It must never generate
metadata from an operationally invalid campaign.

Self-custody evidence supports internal consistency and byte replay; it detects
missing, partial, malformed, or inconsistent artifacts, including re-signed
artifacts without corresponding authority. It provides no independent
raw-response provenance and cannot detect a coherent rewrite of every locally
held artifact.
