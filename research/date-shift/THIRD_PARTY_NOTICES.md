## ExAnte

The pinned upstream repository and dataset revisions are recorded in the study frame and contract. ExAnte's public dataset is identified by its authors as MIT-licensed. This study does not reuse ExAnte model responses, evaluation labels, or reported scores and is not described as a reproduction.

Paper: Yachuan Liu et al. *ExAnte: A Benchmark for Ex-Ante Inference in Large Language Models*. EACL 2026.
