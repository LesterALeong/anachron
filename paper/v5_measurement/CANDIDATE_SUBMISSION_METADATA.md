# Candidate metadata contract

This is a future local metadata contract, not an arXiv upload form. A metadata
generator may run only from a complete verified v5 candidate receipt and the
frozen generated-only manuscript template. It must bind title, author,
abstract, AI-assistance disclosure, category selection, and the explicit v4
inclusion count of zero to the candidate receipt.

The abstract may describe only the controlled synthetic finite panel and may
state generated adherence, conditional-exposure, and eligible paired-contrast
results from the verified answer-free projection. It must disclose that v4 was
an excluded operational pilot and never call v5 outcome-unseen. It must not
expose raw evidence, terminal answers, v4 results, source-audit rationales,
authorization records, reviews, approvals, or outreach text.

Generating local metadata does not authorize researcher contact,
endorsement-code transmission, upload, or submission. Metadata must never be
generated from an operationally invalid campaign.
