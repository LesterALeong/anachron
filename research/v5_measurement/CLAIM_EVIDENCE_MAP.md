# V5 claim and evidence map

V5 may report first-tool adherence and conditional post-cutoff exposure on the controlled eight-card synthetic panel. The exposure denominator contains only first-call-adherent trajectories. Non-adherence is reported by category and is never treated as zero exposure.

V5 does not support answer-quality, population, live-web, source-authenticity, endorsement, or model-ranking claims. V4's operational pilot is disclosed but excluded from all v5 empirical claims.
